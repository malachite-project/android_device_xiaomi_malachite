## Camera and blob provenance: do not upgrade blindly

[14c228be1835](https://github.com/cmdr-chara/android_device_xiaomi_malachite/commit/14c228be1835b244d78fa719df7b578d73e32322) updates OS3.0.10.0 fingerprints/security-patch properties while explicitly retaining **OS2.0.208.0.VOOMIXM userspace blobs because OS3 camera blobs crashed on Global models**. The current `proprietary-files.txt` header still identifies OS2.0.208.0.VOOMIXM unless an entry is noted or pinned. A newer fingerprint does not prove newer binary contents, and a declared security-patch date does not establish fixes in retained binaries.

This is a proprietary Android userspace baseline, **not an instruction to downgrade bootloader, modem or firmware**. Keep those categories separate. Never cross-flash regions or transplant a firmware package to reproduce an extraction baseline. A future camera experiment must identify the exact changed libraries/services, their dependencies, original/fixed hashes and affected SKU; retain a reversible userspace-only candidate and capture the actual HAL tombstone.

## Modernization decision rule

A removal needs the original failure, its current producer/consumer, the upstream replacement at an exact revision, and a test that would fail if the workaround is still needed. Unsupported, unreferenced and obsolete are different states. No camera blob, fingerprint policy, PMF setting, thermal limit, DTBO exclusion or runtime shim was removed during this audit. The only runtime-oriented proposals are separately reviewed product wiring and persistent-autoformat protection; kernel KMI repair is a separate source-build candidate.
